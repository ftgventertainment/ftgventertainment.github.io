Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

ClipForge is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish videos to their own social media accounts. This policy explains what data ClipForge uses and how.

Data ClipForge accesses

When you connect an account, ClipForge receives access tokens from that platform through its official sign-in page. ClipForge never sees or stores your passwords.

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to your channel, and to read your channel's name to show which channel is connected.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to your Instagram Business or Creator account and to read its username.
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to your TikTok account and to read basic profile information.

ClipForge does not access your viewers' data, comments, messages, analytics, subscribers or any other account data.

How the data is used

Access tokens are used only to upload the videos you approve and to show the connection status in your dashboard. They are not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

All tokens, settings and videos are stored only on the computer where ClipForge runs. ClipForge has no central server and does not send your account data anywhere except to the platform it belongs to (for example, YouTube tokens are only sent to Google).

Services ClipForge uses

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

ClipForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To write captions and generate AI videos, ClipForge sends text prompts (not your account data) to Google's Gemini API.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Press Disconnect on the ClipForge Accounts page to delete the stored token for that platform.
  • You can also revoke ClipForge's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting ClipForge's data folder removes everything it has stored.

Stored data is kept only as long as the account stays connected. Video files are deleted automatically 7 days after they are posted.
