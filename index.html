ClipForge

ClipForge is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish short vertical videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

It prepares short videos (highlight clips from creators who have given permission, original narrated stories, and AI-generated videos clearly labelled as such), lets the owner review them, and uploads them on a schedule through each platform's official API.

ClipForge is not offered to the public. Only the account owner and people they personally invite use it.

How ClipForge uses YouTube

ClipForge asks for permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel and to read that channel's name, so the owner can see which channel is connected. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube data.
